# Contact confidence

Use these levels when reporting contact quality.

## C0 - No usable path
- no named person
- no reliable direct path
- only broad website or social presence

## C1 - Weak fallback
- generic inbox only
- contact form only
- switchboard only
- vague team page with no clear owner

## C2 - Plausible but incomplete
- named person found
- role seems current
- LinkedIn profile or company page exists
- no direct email or direct phone found

## C3 - Strong practical path
- named person clearly tied to company now
- direct company email, strong LinkedIn profile, or direct line found
- public evidence is current enough to act on

## C4 - Best-in-class path
- named person confirmed by multiple strong sources
- direct email or direct phone clearly tied to that person
- official source or official PR confirms the route

## Channel notes

### Email
- strongest when listed on official site, official PR, or clearly tied to the named person
- guessed patterns are not higher than C1 unless cross-verified elsewhere

### LinkedIn
- strong when the profile is clearly current and role-relevant
- weak when the name is common or the company match is unclear

### Phone
- strong when it is a direct line, extension, or founder-owner main line for a small business
- weak when it is only a switchboard

### Generic inbox
- useful as a fallback
- not a strong result by itself
