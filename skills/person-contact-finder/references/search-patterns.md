# Search patterns

Use these patterns to find contact paths quickly.

## Company discovery
- `"Company Name" official site`
- `site:company.com about`
- `site:company.com team`
- `site:company.com leadership`
- `site:company.com contact`
- `site:company.com press`
- `site:company.com newsroom`

## Named person verification
- `"Person Name" "Company Name"`
- `site:linkedin.com/in "Person Name" "Company Name"`
- `site:company.com "Person Name"`
- `site:company.com press "Person Name"`
- `site:company.com newsroom "Person Name"`

## Contact path discovery
- `site:company.com "@company.com"`
- `site:company.com contact`
- `site:company.com press email`
- `site:company.com media contact`
- `site:company.com team email`
- `"Person Name" "@company.com"`
- `"Person Name" email "Company Name"`
- `"Company Name" phone marketing`

## Good stopping rule
Stop when you have:
- one strong named person and one strong path
- or one strong fallback plus clear evidence no better path is public

Do not keep searching just to produce more names.
