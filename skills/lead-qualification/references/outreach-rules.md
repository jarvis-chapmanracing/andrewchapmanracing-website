# Outreach Rules

Use these rules when deciding whether a lead is ready and what path matters.

## First principle
Do not start with people. Start with companies.

Sequence:
1. find target company
2. confirm the company is worth pursuing
3. identify the best person based on company size
4. identify the best contact path
5. decide whether the lead is pitch-ready

## Route quality
Best to worst:
1. direct company email
2. LinkedIn
3. phone number
4. Instagram DM

Do not treat these as sufficient for a strong lead by themselves:
- contact form
- generic company email

## Contact selection logic
- smaller company -> founder or owner first
- bigger startup or mid-size brand -> CMO or marketing director first
- fallback -> CEO, president, other marketing staff, other clear decision-makers
- weak fallback -> sales manager or another relevant manager

## Readiness test
A lead is outreach-ready only when:
- the company fit is confirmed
- the right person is identified
- a usable contact path is identified
- the deal looks practical enough to propose

## Important caution
Do not mistake "a company exists" for "a company is a lead."
A lead only becomes high quality when the company is worth pursuing and the right contact path is established.
