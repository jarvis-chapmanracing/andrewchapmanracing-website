# Search patterns

Use these patterns to find people quickly.

## Company discovery
- `"Company Name" official site`
- `site:company.com about`
- `site:company.com team`
- `site:company.com leadership`
- `site:company.com contact`
- `site:company.com press`
- `site:company.com newsroom`

## Person discovery by role
- `"Company Name" founder`
- `"Company Name" owner`
- `"Company Name" CEO`
- `"Company Name" president`
- `"Company Name" "marketing director"`
- `"Company Name" "brand partnerships"`
- `"Company Name" sponsorship`
- `"Company Name" "community manager"`

## Named person verification
- `"Person Name" "Company Name"`
- `site:linkedin.com/in "Person Name" "Company Name"`
- `site:company.com "Person Name"`
- `site:company.com press "Person Name"`
- `site:company.com newsroom "Person Name"`

## Good stopping rule
Stop when you have:
- the main decision-maker group covered
- the likely outreach-owner group covered
- and no clear signal that more high-value names are being missed

Do not keep searching just to produce more names.
